Network settings⌄

The SUNRACER_USE_SOCKS_TUNNEL=1 launch option currently forces 127.0.0.1:1080. Restart without that option to apply your saved preference.

These settings affect the native client only. They do not start an SSH tunnel or change GNOME's system proxy.
